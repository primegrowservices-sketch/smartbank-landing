FAQs

Before You Apply

Is loan approval guaranteed?

No. Approval depends on the concerned lender's eligibility, verification and applicable terms.

What affects the loan amount?

Income, turnover, credit profile, existing obligations, property details where applicable and lender criteria can affect eligibility.

What documents are required?

Documents vary by product and lender. KYC, income, banking and supporting documents may be required.

Can I ask about low CIBIL?

Yes. Share your profile and requirement. Available options can be discussed, but approval cannot be guaranteed.
